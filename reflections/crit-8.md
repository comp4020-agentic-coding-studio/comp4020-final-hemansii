# Crit 8: It's alive!

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough was when I stopped treating the project as just a grid where people dig for random artefacts and gave the digging a bigger purpose. Changing it into a shared excavation of one forgotten website made everything else make more sense: the specimen tray, field notes, theories and the idea of people coming back to continue what others started.

The second breakthrough was with the design. I went through a few visual directions that I really did not like, and eventually realised the problem was not individual icons or colours, it was that the whole site did not feel like one place. Once I started designing it as an excavation scene rather than a dashboard, the layout and interactions became much clearer.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

It made me realise I want to get better at recognising when an idea technically works but still does not feel right. A lot of the progress this week came from looking at the result, deciding I did not like it, and being specific about why instead of just accepting the first version.

I want to be better at directing that iteration: keeping the parts that work, changing the parts that do not, and making sure every new feature actually strengthens the main idea instead of just adding more things to the page.
