# Crit 9: All at once

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough was realising that making the data update live was not enough to make the game feel multiplayer. At first, I was mainly thinking about whether one person's dig or note appeared on another person's screen without refreshing. Once that worked, I started thinking more about how another person's presence should actually feel.

That changed the direction of the features. Player names became visible across digs, notes and theories, changes animate when they arrive, and I added notifications when another player leaves a field note or theory while you are online. If two people try to dig the same tile, the second player also gets told that someone else got there first. That made the real-time system feel like part of the game rather than just a technical requirement.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

It made me think more about what a technical feature actually feels like from the user's side. Something can be technically real-time and still feel invisible or confusing.

I also found myself correcting the agent much more visually this week. I rejected layouts, shadows, assets and interactions that technically worked but did not feel right. I want to get better at spotting those moments and clearly explaining what needs to change, rather than stopping once something simply works.
